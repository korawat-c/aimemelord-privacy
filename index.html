Thai Dubber Uploader

Internal upload tool for the Aimemelord YouTube channel

Thai Dubber Uploader is a private, internal tool operated by Korawat Charoenpitaks. It publishes Thai-dubbed episodes to the operator's own YouTube channel: it uploads each finished video, sets its thumbnail, adds it to the series playlist and checks the status of the operator's own uploads. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Watch on YouTube: @aimemelordz

YouTube API Services

This tool uses YouTube API Services (YouTube Data API v3) with the operator's own Google account via OAuth 2.0. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service, the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
