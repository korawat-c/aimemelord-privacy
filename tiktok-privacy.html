Privacy Policy – 3AM Stories

Last updated: 9 November 2026

"3AM Stories" is a private publishing tool operated by AI Memelord. It uploads short videos that we produce ourselves (original Thai horror stories, the 3AM Stories channel) to TikTok accounts that we own. It is not offered to the public and no other TikTok user can sign in to it.

What we collect

  • When we connect one of our own TikTok accounts, TikTok gives the tool an access token and refresh token, the account's open ID, and basic profile information (display name and avatar) needed to confirm which account is connected.
  • The upload status that TikTok returns for each video we post (publish ID, status).

We do not collect information about any other TikTok user, viewers, followers, comments, messages or analytics of other accounts.

How we use it

Only to upload our own videos (as drafts to the account's inbox, or as posts) to the connected account, and to check that each upload finished.

Storage and sharing

Tokens are stored on our own server, readable only by the upload tool, and never shown in logs. We do not sell, rent or share any data with anyone. No advertising or tracking is used.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the account stays connected. Revoke access at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services), or email us and we delete the stored tokens within 7 days.

Children

The tool is not directed to children and is used only by its operator.

Changes

Changes are published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
